Mysteria Privacy Policy

Offline-first Scriptural Rosary app

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Mysteria is an offline-first Scriptural Rosary app.

Data Collection

Mysteria does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Mysteria does not require an account. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or social features.

Local App Data

Mysteria stores a small amount of app data locally on your device so the app can work as expected. This may include:

  • text size preferences
  • local reading history used to select unseen scriptural readings
  • other local app preferences

This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer or to a third party.

You can delete this local data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions And Network Access

The production Android version of Mysteria does not request Internet access and does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, or files.

Development builds may use Internet access for Flutter development features such as debugging and hot reload. This does not apply to the production release distributed through Google Play.

Children

Mysteria does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as Mysteria changes. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.
